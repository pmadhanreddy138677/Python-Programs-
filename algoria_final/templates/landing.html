Our Mission

Your Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

What are the prerequisites?

None. No prior Python experience is required — Beginner level starts from what a computer even is.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Telugu (తెలుగు), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી) and Marathi (मराठी). You can change your language at any time.

What's the difference between the three modes?

Animation Mode is a guided, step-by-step walkthrough. Theory & Practice gives you notes plus two quick questions. Game Mode is five scored mini tasks worth 50 points total.

Do I need to install anything?

No — everything runs in the browser. A separate Tkinter desktop app exists with the exact same lessons if you'd rather learn offline.
