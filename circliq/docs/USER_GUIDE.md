# User guide

## Students
1. **Sign up** with your university email and verify it (the link arrives by email). Finish the short onboarding: photo, interests, skills, goals, and join a few communities.
2. **Find people**: *Discover* (search/filters) or *Campus Match* (Friend, Study, Project suggestions with reasons). Send a connection request; once connected you can message each other.
3. **Join communities** and post discussions, questions or project ideas. React, comment, save, or report anything inappropriate.
4. **Study and build**: post a *study request* to form a group; start a *Circle* around a shared goal; browse the *Project Hub* and apply to projects that need your skills.
5. **Ask Circliq**: ask campus questions and answer others. Accepted answers and helpful votes earn contribution badges (recognition inside Circliq only).
6. **Events and faculty**: register for events and save them; find mentors in *Faculty Connect*.
7. **Your privacy** (*Settings & privacy*): hide yourself from Discover, leave Campus Match, block people, download your data, deactivate or delete your account.

## Faculty
After you register as faculty, an administrator approves your account. Then you can: complete your faculty profile (expertise, office hours), choose whether students may message you or ask for mentorship, post announcements, publish mentorship/research opportunities and official events.

## Administrators
Open **Campus admin** from the sidebar: statistics, user list (approve or decline faculty, restrict or restore accounts with a reason), official community badges, reports overview, and the audit log. You cannot read anyone's private messages. Community moderators handle reports in their own community's *Moderation* page; Ask Circliq reports are in the admin's Ask queue.

## If something is wrong
Use the report (flag) button on content, or contact a campus administrator.
