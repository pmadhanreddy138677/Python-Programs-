How does matching work? Transparent by design

Scores come from simple, visible rules, never a hidden model. Each point maps to a reason shown on the card.

Friend Match: shared interests (hobbies count extra), wanting new friends, overlapping free time, same year.
Study Match: the same subjects, wanting study partners, overlapping free time, same department and year.
Project Match: skills you don't have, shared skills, wanting teammates, collaboration style, tech interests.

Only information people entered themselves is used. Nothing sensitive is inferred, and anyone can leave Campus Match from their profile settings.
